## Recommended implementation scope for approval

Fix 1-6 together within the standard new-appointment review. Use existing CSS, tokens, controls and retained form state. No dependencies, new imagery or backend changes are needed. Preserve current submission and approval behavior. Make content and wrapping correct first, then add section correction controls and staff typography scope.

Suggested composition: a clear review heading and short instruction; visit section with destination, localized WITA schedule and purpose; representative section with separate organization, email, phone and identity type; roster with total and one name per item; supporting-document filename or explicit no-document text; existing final action with a secondary Back action.

Acceptance: all entered details can be checked; section correction retains input; labels and values have distinct hierarchy; full values wrap at 320px; no horizontal dialog overflow with long email/filename or a 20-person roster; final action remains reachable; focus returns to the appropriate section; existing role and submit guards remain intact.
